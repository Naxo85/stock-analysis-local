STRENGTH EVIDENCE41 — same rubric, independent reasons, no score target

Only new preparations >=41 use this contract. Frozen <=40 retain their exact
instructions, schemas, receipts and dated assessments. Apply the existing weights,
subcriteria, volume formula and financial authority independently of visibility.
This clarification does not itself prove a prior error or authorize rescoring.

Separate a changed support/zone, a demonstrated change in market backing, a
demonstrated prior scoring error, a changed measurement/coverage, and a material
update to what is now known about the same support. New coverage does not prove
that contracts were opened or that an earlier unknown was zero. It can nevertheless
resolve a material limitation in the present judgment: identify the limited prior
premise, the applicable new observation and why its information changes the affected
component. Use the ordinary authorized change_review basis and exact sources;
do not invent a market change or prior error. Reappearing data, duplicated rows or
another presentation of already incorporated facts do not earn points again.

Distinguish the questions answered by the existing components:
- Structure assesses objective definition, noise, technical horizons and precision.
- Volume/reaction assesses the supplied profile and completed historical reactions
  under the existing mechanical rubric. A pending observation is not a completed
  defense, failed defense or automatic loss of an otherwise viable buying role.
- Options assesses the applicable observed contracts, relative concentration,
  neighbours, distance, quote quality and persistence in the sampled expirations.
- Independent confluence assesses genuinely different evidence that coincides at
  the support. Identify the additional information supplied by each family.
- Durability assesses persistence and resilience of the WHOLE support, including
  evidence independent of a particular contract, event or recent price reaction.

One fact may inform different questions, but its consequences must be distinct.
Do not propagate the same unknown or limitation as an automatic deduction across
components. Historical sample size, recency and event survival are different
questions; repeating the phrase 'few defenses' does not explain a separate expiry
risk. A genuine event or demonstrated loss can affect several dimensions when
each affected judgment has its own supported consequence. Do not suppress real
risk merely to make the components appear independent.

For confluence, duplicate providers, overlapping lists, several strikes/expiries
of the same option concentration, or transformations of the same price history
do not create independent families just by having different names. A pivot, gap,
moving average and reaction from the same prices may describe useful structure
or horizons; their diversity alone is not independent corroboration. An AVWAP
shares price/volume inputs: do not count it again as an independent copy of both.
An independently informative measurement of traded activity or observed contracts
can contribute when it actually adds evidence at the zone; shared raw inputs do
not make every distinct measurement useless. Explain the additional information,
not a count of labels. Keep the existing confluence subcriteria and maximum;
there is no new fixed table of points by family count or mandatory family quota.

Missing profile, unavailable reaction, unobserved contract, zero observed OI,
pending forward window and demonstrated failed defense are different states.
Use the available valid evidence without inventing points for the missing part.
Unknown is not adverse observation and does not automatically invalidate the
support, remove other families or reduce all components. Missing contract
liquidity is not evidence that a price-based support expires. When contracts
expire, assess their actual remaining contributions in Options; reduce survival
of the whole support only to the extent it depends on that expiry or another
identified event. Independent structure/volume and later contracts may survive.

When an inherited rationale is relevant to the ordinary review, do not use an
unidentified dealer-position sign or calls-minus-puts proxy as a currently proved
adverse mechanism. Check whether the remaining observable evidence still supports
the affected judgment. Retain it when justified; correct an established error or
materially changed judgment through the ordinary evidence-bound authority. Removing
an unsupported causal statement does not automatically increase its component.
The introduction of this policy alone is not proof of prior_error. Preserve the
dated original as history, not live authority for the unsupported mechanism.

available; never treat a switch in the dominant label as expiration of all backing.
Do not invent old per-expiry distributions for comparison.
