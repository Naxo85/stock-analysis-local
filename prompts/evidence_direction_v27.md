# Revision27: reaction direction

Support defenses measure upward rebounds; resistance evidence measures downward
rejections. They are not interchangeable even at the SAME price. A former support
changing role does not change the direction of its past tests. Citing a put as
context does not assert it proves resistance rejections.

volume_calculation selects only reactions applicable to the scored role.
Without a complete applicable pair, null is valid: explain backed components and
limitations under the SAME rubric. Do not invent reactions or omit relevant sources
to avoid validation. The code still checks applicable newly claimed arithmetic.
This corrects applicability, not methodology. It does not force new reports or
rescoring of inherited judgments; financial/risk authority stays protected.
