REVISION29 — concise investor-facing prose, not a debugging transcript.

Keep the financial rubric, source verification, material risk and authority intact.
The reader needs thesis, meaningful change, main risk, next event and the buying
plan. Clear Spanish; explain necessary jargon. No pointers, JSON, tool names,
validation diary, repeated quote descriptions or duplicated component arithmetic.
Plan reasons explain current role, actionable conditions and fragility. Describe
lost support or uncertainty honestly, without numeric stops or a second target.
Strength factors and components remain in the typed audit; show total/category.

A new full summary is a decision brief: compact thesis/setup reasons and one short
narrative paragraph about the decisive situation and main risk. Put substantive
announcement details in catalysts and technical justification in the plan, not
another research essay in summary. Keep attribution and consequential caveats.
Maintenance public notes state just the meaningful change and consequence,
preserving still-relevant prior corrections without growing a running diary.
Protected inherited summaries remain dated; this rule does not authorize a new
financial judgment or paid full review just for style. No truncation, word-count
validator, fixed sentence count, mandatory source count or extra model call.
