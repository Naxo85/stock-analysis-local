AUTHORING BUDGETS (new or substantively edited public text, BOTH roles)
A logical line means one short sentence, not a browser's physical wrapped line.
These are maximum writing budgets, not targets to fill. Headings, the separately
rendered prices/scores/dates and source URLs do not consume the prose budgets.
- summary.thesis_reason: 1 sentence, 20 words.
- summary.setup_reason: 1 sentence, 20 words.
- summary.narrative: 3 short sentences, 60 words total.
- Each plan reason (Entry, Washout, Resistance): 2 short sentences, 45 words total.
- Each catalyst explanation: 2 short sentences, 40 words total; a short title.
- Next event explanation: 2 short sentences, 35 words total; keep date/event.
- Conclusion: 2 short sentences, 35 words total.
- An authorized maintenance public_note: 2 short sentences, 35 words total.
Use less when possible. Do not evade the budgets with huge sentences, compressed
jargon, semicolon chains, abbreviations or prose stuffed into titles/link labels.
Within this SAME response, edit your draft to fit before returning the JSON.
No separate editor/model call, paid style repair or style-based escalation.

WHAT BELONGS WHERE
Summary = current business thesis versus price driver, decisive risk and stance.
Explain a score change in its own short reason, not again in the narrative.
Catalysts = meaningful facts, dated with their consequence and supporting links;
distinguish forecasts from realized facts and old continuing drivers from news.
Do not arbitrarily cap the number of material catalysts or drop adverse evidence.
Plan = what currently supports THIS zone and the relevant condition/fragility.
Prefer plain Spanish: buying/selling concentration rather than unexplained OI,
gamma, HVN or AVWAP. A technical term is useful only with a brief explanation.
The renderer already shows ranges, quote position and strength: do not repeat
their arithmetic, full timestamps, ATR distances or all candidate rankings.
Conclusion = the ticker-specific decision and condition, not our operating manual.

Comparisons with CURRENT, rejected alternatives, continuity, why a proposal changed,
rubric components, calculations, research provenance and unsuccessful searches
belong in existing INTERNAL reasoning, factors, assessments/change_review/evidence.
Do not add output fields or publish a research/debugging diary. Full analysis
still performs those checks; maintenance still supplies its required reviews.
Explain a correction publicly only when needed to understand the financial
decision, in the affected section, not repeatedly throughout the report.
A newly requested operational resistance is a change of purpose, not evidence
that the earlier structural resistance was an analytical error or of fresh news.
Explain the new supply zone and its consequence without inventing either claim.
Do not repeat generic rules ('never accumulate entries', 'no reclaim', 'not an
automatic sale', 'we retain unless broken') as ticker-specific conclusions.

UNCERTAINTY WITHOUT NOISE
Do not list unverified rumors, accusations, abandoned searches or inaccessible
pages merely because you investigated them. Never imply that these are absent,
false or harmless just because verification failed. When a limitation affects
the decision/confidence, state that specific uncertainty concisely in the relevant
section, and keep actual risk/blocked/escalation authority intact. Attribution,
forecast qualifications and material counterevidence must survive compression.

For an above-quote buying zone, briefly state affirmative evidence of CURRENT
support viability and what remains unconfirmed; 'no recorded break', old strength
or a missing failed retest is not sufficient. Do not declare it lost mechanically
from distance, a marginal quote crossing or the wish to make the prose shorter.
Assess chronology/current independent backing; remove genuinely lost supports,
never rescue them as reclaim entries. Unknown essential evidence stays unknown.

INTEGRITY AND OLD TEXT
Budgets govern writing, not financial validation. Never truncate output or hide
overflow in CSS, fail a ticker for word/sentence counts, weaken financial checks,
force full Sol, or make another model call just for style. Never sacrifice an
essential risk qualification or invent certainty to shorten a sentence.
Unedited inherited text stays EXACT and dated, even if long. Maintenance cannot
rewrite protected thesis/narrative/catalysts/event, redate history or publish a
style-only update. Apply budgets to new full reports and otherwise authorized
substantive maintenance text edits. Do not claim old reports were shortened.
