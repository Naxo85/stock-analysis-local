REVISION28 — an executive summary is a decision brief, not the research report.

This editorial rule applies when full Sol WRITES a new summary and when maintenance
writes an authorized public note. It replaces the older broad exposition guidance
for these fields, without weakening research, factual verification or authority.
The investigation can be extensive; the text shown to the investor must be selective.

For full Sol, summary.thesis_reason briefly explains the business judgment and
summary.setup_reason briefly explains trading attractiveness. Each is a compact
reason, not a second narrative. summary.narrative is one short paragraph covering
the decisive current situation/change and main risk or caveat. Together these
fields should read at a glance, not as a sequence of research findings. Do not
repeat the scores, technical plan or business explanation already stated there.

Place announcement dates, product/customer detail and insider-event distinctions
in catalysts when material; put entry/removal/stop rationale in plan. Put source
selection, calculations, rejected hypotheses and research limitations without a
decision consequence in the internal audit, not the executive summary. Do not
catalogue every searched subject, minor headline or generic unverified risk.
If a missing fact changes confidence or the decision, retain that caveat clearly.
Keep attribution next to claims; concision never authorizes unsupported certainty.

Maintenance public_note states only the reader-relevant change/correction and its
consequence, concisely. Merge still-relevant prior corrections without appending a
running diary. Internal assessments carry review detail. Do not rewrite protected
summary/narrative, change report dates or escalate/pay for full Sol just for style.

This is a writing instruction, NOT a word-count validator, truncation algorithm,
required sentence count, obligatory update, or additional model call. Historical
text stays exact until an authorized substantive edit or natural full report.
