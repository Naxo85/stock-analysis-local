SUPPORT ORIGINS41 — directed observations of authenticated active origins

levels.support_origins observes exact gap_up_lower and range_bottom_3_6m
identities cited by active CURRENT Entry/Washout, alongside the existing directed
AVWAP and option observations. It uses the current capture's supplied history;
it does not broaden the history, add a market-data request or replace the ranked
shortlist. Read requested_identity, status, observation_reason, observation_scope
and the supplied source_record/evidence. Match the exact type, level, date and
other identity fields rather than guessing an index, substitute date or nearest
coordinate. A level at the same price is not necessarily the same origin.

available means the requested historical origin was identified and observed with
the stated current data. It does not mean that the origin still works as buying
support, was newly defended, is safe, or has any minimum strength. A previously
formed gap or range can remain identifiable after losing its buying role; inspect
its actual current record, fill/closure state where supplied, completed sessions,
independent backing and material risks. Observation never forces retention.

not_observed is scoped to the supplied reconstruction pool and identity; it does
not prove that the origin never existed, has broken or has disappeared globally.
unavailable retains the stated history, identity or ambiguity limitation. Do not
convert either status into zero OI, zero reactions, an automatic score deduction
or loss of the buying role. Essential unresolved evidence can still block a safe
decision. Missing optional detail alone is not essential_data or a full route.

The directed row and a matching shortlisted row describe the same origin, not
two independent supports or evidence families. A reference row supplies dated
identity/context, never current backing. Use the exact current source_catalog
handle and inspect the complete selected record. Negative observations may
explain limits but cannot supply a positive live origin for a replacement zone.

Newly observed provenance can materially improve current knowledge under
REVISION41; it does not by itself prove a new market event, prior_error or a need
to rescore. Apply the same viability, novelty, applicability and component rules
to directed and shortlisted sources. Preserve valid exact ranges and judgments,
withdraw genuinely lost buying roles, and leave a slot absent when no suitable
support exists. No forced selection, quota, score uplift, visibility target,
extra model/research call, new repair, changed weight or automatic retention.
