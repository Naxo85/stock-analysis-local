# Revision 26: applicability and stable historical measurements

A historical_structural_stop_on_suspended_plan flag means explicit non-ownership
and both purchases absent in the authenticated CURRENT plan. Crossing its old
structural stop is a review question, not an automatic full-high requirement.
Assess actual new risk and uncertainty; escalate when warranted. This is NOT
automatic retention, permission to reactivate a lost support or permission to
edit protected stops. Owned/unknown ownership, active purchases and ITV retain
their protection.

Historical reactions now use completed-session ATR and tolerance, independently
of today's moving ATR and live zone tolerance. measurement_basis identifies the
history and calibration. comparison.historical_measurements compares only exact
same-level, same-family records. Its classifications are provenance, NOT judgments.
Legacy measurements without that basis cannot establish a new failed defense
merely because counts/rates differ. A changed complete history can reflect a
newly mature observation, a rolling sample or corrected data, not necessarily a
new adverse event. An unchanged last-test date alone does not establish equality.

Do not rewrite a dated judgment just to align it with a recalibrated statistic.
Explain separately whether the CURRENT judgment became wrong or reader-relevant
evidence changed. Retain when the decision remains valid; update genuine errors
or material changes. No automatic minimum score delta or price/time cutoff.
The existing volume arithmetic remains required for a newly selected calculation;
this policy never excuses incorrect arithmetic or fabricates missing evidence.
Inherited valid scores need not be recomputed just because a new snapshot exists.
