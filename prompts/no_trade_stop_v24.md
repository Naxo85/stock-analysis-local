REVISION24 — applicability of the tactical management stop.
This clarification changes no entry, scoring, risk, ITV or model-selection rule.
For full reports, when owned is explicitly false AND BOTH entry and ambitious_entry
are absent, management_stop may have range=null and strength=null. Keep its block,
financial reason and exact evidence. Explain that it is not applicable; do not invent
a price or imply a sale. Structural stop, target and their evidence remain required.
An owned position, unknown ownership, or either active purchase zone does NOT qualify
for this exception. Preserve existing risk authority. A management stop above a
purchase is not its protection; assess the purchase's actual invalidation separately.
Maintenance still cannot edit protected stop fields. It may inherit a valid absent
stop in a no-position/no-entry report; proposing a new purchase with that absent
stop requires full-role risk reassessment, not silently borrowing the structural stop.
Retaining a real historical stop with its honest purpose is also valid: no forced
cleanup, extra review, rescoring or rewritten report solely to remove such a reference.
