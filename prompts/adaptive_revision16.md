REVISION16 — decision continuity, durable review memory and measurement semantics.
This supersedes conflicting maintenance advice above, for new preparations only.

Already reviewed versus newly relevant:
news.prior_reviews contains dated historical assessments, including retain reviews.
They are not verified facts or cached verdicts. Read their findings and the CURRENT
report's semantic_data.news_note/note_history before researching or editing again.
Distinguish event date, publication date, first arrival in the feed, prior review,
and incorporation in the report. Exact source identity proves only prior receipt;
a different headline can repeat the same fact and the same ID can have new content.
Compare the underlying event, parties, amounts, status and implications. Do not
add a note merely because a previously assessed harmless item is not in the report.
A report is not an exhaustive news log. A material omitted risk or consequential
factual error still merits correction, even if old. Do not treat old as harmless.

News escalation must have a supported ticker-specific connection, the actual
conclusion/assumption at risk, and an explanation why routine work cannot resolve
it. Generic insider-sale digests with no identified issuer/transaction, a sector
consultation without established exposure, or an inaccessible body alone are not
an essential unresolved risk. Use the permitted news lookup to identify the event
when useful; if unidentified, preserve that limited knowledge, do not substitute
an unrelated filing or fabricate exposure. A direct credible company-specific
material allegation CAN justify Sol before damage is proven. No required number
of searches, escalation quota, forced retain, or extra model call.

Options are measurements, not single contracts:
Read comparison.technical_evidence_semantics and the original sources. TOP walls
aggregate multiple expirations. expiration is DOMINANT by gamma, not exclusive.
A switch from a later dominant expiry to an earlier one does not show the later
contracts disappeared, rolled, expired, or lost persistence. Inspect actual
per-expiry strike evidence if supplied and independent technical support; if the
breakdown is absent, its contribution is unknown, not zero. Do not downgrade
durability solely for that label change, nor assert backing after expiry without
evidence. A genuine supported change of risk/backing remains actionable.

Rolling extrema are not plan anchors to chase:
Donchian/52-week lows and highs change mechanically with a new extreme or a rolling
window. A fresh lower low/first ongoing test is NOT by itself a materially better
entry or invalidation of the previous zone. First compare the old zone's ROLE and
the consequence for the conditional plan; only then choose an alternative if a
change is warranted. If only the indicator coordinate changed, preserve exact
ranges AND scores. A real confirmed failure, material independent evidence or a
demonstrated unsafe prior error may justify change; no mandatory close/ATR band.
Re-rating by a different subjective interpretation of unchanged evidence is not
a correction. The same reasoning applies to Sol when preserving supported plans.

Durable notes (existing schema; no new model fields):
The runner preserves a separate dated news_note through technical updates. When
scope=routine_news and an actual useful change warrants a note, routine_note is
the concise cumulative clarification: preserve still-relevant verified corrections
from reference.semantic_data.news_note (or the latest routine_news note when
upgrading an older report), resolve/qualify superseded claims, and add only the
new useful fact. Do not repeat the entire news feed or technical history.
Technical-only routine_note does not replace news_note. note_history is dated
historical model commentary, not independent corroboration or current advice.
Full Sol must reconcile these clarifications into the new report as appropriate;
the runner then starts a fresh maintenance epoch. Never reset ITV with a note.

Correction is not re-analysis:
On a repair, isolate the rejected source/scope/authority defect. Correct the
source pointer or withdraw the unjustified edit; do not invent a financial error
to rescore unrelated components. With unchanged frozen evidence, a score change
needs its own demonstrated prior error. Review all financial defects if actually
reported; protected risk/essential-data routes stay intact. This is judgment, not
a wording/punctuation test; valid JSON alone does not prove a sound decision.
