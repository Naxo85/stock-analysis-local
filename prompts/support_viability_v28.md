REVISION28 — assess the buying role before preserving a historical judgment.

This decision order governs BOTH roles, including repair. Continuity protects
VALID decisions, not decisions merely unchallenged by a previous reviewer.
It takes precedence over an instruction to retain because prices/evidence are
unchanged. It does not widen maintenance authority or alter the financial rubric.

For each CURRENT Entry and Washout, assess present buying-support viability using
slim.price_history (recent completed-session OHLCV), the separate current quote
and its quality, the zone's original basis and current independent backing.
Explain that judgment in the existing technical assessment, before considering
an alternative or score. Distinguish viable support, lost buying role, and genuinely
unresolved essential evidence. Do not manufacture intraday ordering from daily
bars. The supplied window is limited; do not invent earlier sessions.

A marginal penetration can leave support viable: judge the observed price path,
reactions, structure and quote uncertainty together. Do not automatically delete
or rescore it from quote location, percentage/ATR distance, a fixed number of
closes, list absence or the existence of a lower alternative. Conversely, options
OI, an AVWAP coordinate and an old strength score do not alone establish that a
zone still works as buying support after price has moved below it.

Historical_reaction's five-session forward window measures historical rebounds.
pending_test_count / an immature forward window is NOT a grace period during
which an active entry must survive. Evaluate the observed subsequent sessions
already available; do not wait for five future sessions to assess current role.
Nor does no recorded failed_retest prove viability. A previous retain judgment
is an opinion to check, not evidence or a veto on correcting a prior error.

Keep an above-quote zone only with a defensible present support judgment, not
solely 'no proof of break', 'it was strong', 'Sol chose it' or 'wait for recovery'.
Where the evidence instead establishes a lost buying role, remove that active
entry even if its historic strength remains high. A future reclaim of a lost
zone is a different strategy and cannot rescue this buy-the-dip field. Review
lower viable alternatives; if none is defensible, leave range/strength absent.
Do not require another entry before removing a lost one. Do not infer a loss
from missing essential data: blocked is distinct from an absent trade.

For maintenance, use existing change_review: backing_changed for actual observed
change; prior_error for a demonstrably mistaken inherited buying-role judgment.
Cite the dated CURRENT judgment and evidence, not a fictional new market event.
Unchanged price does not prevent correcting a wrong active role. Keep unaffected
prices/scores/text exact; reconcile affected public reasons and Setup only when
financially necessary. Structural invalidation, protected stops, ownership risk,
critical uncertainty and ITV retain existing full-role authority. No style gate,
new automatic routing guard or extra model stage is introduced.
